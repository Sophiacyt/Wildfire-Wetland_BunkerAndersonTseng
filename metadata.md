All Datasets are accessible for public data download as of October 2026.

Expected Variables include (and are subject to grow over time as we further explore the data):
- boolean T/F for fire crossing a wetland
- fire proximity to the closest wetland

Spatial Coverage is limited to the state of California.

Temporal Coverage is initially limited to 2025 and may expand as we develop functions.

=======
The CA Wetlands is a direct download from the USFWS National Wetland Inventory by Mason Bunker on 10/19/2026. Metadata from NWI can be accessed from the Wetlands Mapper tool. Raw Data is project in equal-area Albers projection for the 1983 North American Data with meters as a unit. Project data has been reprojected to UTM Zone 10N.
=======
California Historic Fire Perimeters: https://www.fire.ca.gov/what-we-do/fire-resource-assessment-program/fire-perimeters
Documents fires greater or equal to 10 acres inside the state of CA between 1910 and 2025 by Year, Reporting Agency, Unit ID, Fire Name, Local Incident Number, Alarm Date, Containment Date, Cause, Collection Method, Management Objective, GIS Calculated Acres. Dataset limitations include duplicate fires reported by multiple agencies, missing historical records, and fires below minimum size cutoff. Origina data was projected in the California Albers Projection (EPSG:3310) but has been reprojected to EPSG:26910, UTM Zone 10N.
Discovered errors may be reported to kimberly.wallin@fire.ca.gov

All Datasets are accessible for public data download as of October 2026.

Expected Variables include (and are subject to grow over time as we further explore the data):
- boolean T/F for fire crossing a wetland
- fire proximity to the closest wetland

Spatial Coverage is limited to the state of California.

Temporal Coverage is initially limited to 2025 and may expand as we develop functions.

=======
The CA Wetlands is a direct download from the USFWS National Wetland Inventory by Mason Bunker on 10/19/2026. Metadata from NWI can be accessed from the Wetlands Mapper tool. Raw Data is project in equal-area Albers projection for the 1983 North American Data with meters as a unit.
=======
California Historic Fire Perimeters: https://www.fire.ca.gov/what-we-do/fire-resource-assessment-program/fire-perimeters
- Fires greater or equal to 10 acres inside the state of CA between 1910 and 2025
- Documents Year, Reporting Agency, Unit ID, Fire Name, Local Incident Number, Alarm Date, Containment Date, Cause, Collection Method, Management Objective, GIS Calculated Acres
- Dataset limitations include duplicate fires reported by multiple agencies, missing historical records, and fires below minimum size cutoff
- Discovered errors may be reported to kimberly.wallin@fire.ca.gov

Data Quality Audit: Our biggest challenge with the fire dataset will be dealing with all of the missing values, most of which are associated with the shapefile. As time allows, the CalFire website will be revisited to see if a more complete dataset exists, because the CSV contains more fires, but without the available footprints needed to compare to spatial distribution of wetlands. If a shapefile without missing IRWINID exists, that would be extremely helpful for the join because all fires are given a unique IRWINID.

We began with (rows,columns): (521, 21) and fire perimeter records: (11139, 22), but after joining we are left with (243, 14). Some columns with information deemed unimportant that also contained missing values or were duplicates were dropped: 'GIS Calculated Acres','OBJECTID', 'Unit ID', 'Local Incident Number', 'IRWIN ID', 'Comments', 'GlobalID', 'Fire Number (historical use)','Complex ID', 'Complex Name'.

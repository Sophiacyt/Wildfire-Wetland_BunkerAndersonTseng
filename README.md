# Wildfire Wetland_BunkerAndersonTseng
Group Project - Wildfire &amp; Wetland
Group project about wildfire & wetland

Team Name: Wildfire and Wetland Interactions in California
Team Members: Kat Anderson, Mason Bunker, Sophia Tseng
Research Question: What role do wetlands plan in wildfire movement across the landscape? Are wildfires less frequent near wetlands? Has wetland loss occured due to wildfires? Do fires cross wetland boundaries?
Intended Audience: Wildfire and wetland researchers.
